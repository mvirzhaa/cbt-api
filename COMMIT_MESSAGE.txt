💡 Scoring Algorithm (TIPE_2):
Formula: max(0, (correct - wrong) / total_keys) × bobot
- Full score if all correct and no wrong answers
- Partial score based on proportion
- Minimum score is 0 (no negative scores)

Example:
Key: A,C,E (3 correct)
Answer: A,B,C (2 correct, 1 wrong)
Score: (2 - 1) / 3 × 10 = 3.33
